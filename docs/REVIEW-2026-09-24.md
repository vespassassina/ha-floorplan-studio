# Review, 2026-09-24: the tool against the field

Scope: ha-floorplan-studio 0.10.3, the editor and the card, looked at by
hand (`npm run shots`, the built `dist/editor.html` at desktop and phone
width), and the competing tools and forum threads as of today. Star counts
were read from the GitHub API on the day. Reddit could not be searched, so
the user voice below comes from the Home Assistant community forum and
GitHub issues, about twenty threads in all. Ranking there is judgement,
not a count.

Smaller 2025–2026 entrants: hacs-floorplan (2D/3D/pixel-art), HA-Floors
and ha-floorplan-zone-card (both built around zoom), ha-explorer-card. The
field is crowded and moves weekly. We are five days old with zero stars.

## What people ask for, most often first

1. **No YAML, no SVG, no GIMP.** The loudest ask by far.
2. **Change a device without rebuilding everything.** Render pipelines regenerate every light mask.
3. **Usable on a phone: zoom, pan, fit.** Asked of the Android app in 2021, of picture-elements in 2026, still open.
4. **Realistic light.** Colour, glow, day and night. Easy Floorplan's sun work gets the most engagement it has.
5. **Things that move.** Vacuums, mmWave targets, person trackers. The most-commented open issue on Easy Floorplan; 30 comments on Houseplan's radar issue.
6. **Fine geometry.** Exact dimensions, plain openings.
7. **One plan, every device, offline.** Complaints about settings saved per browser.
8. **History replay.** 24 comments on Easy Floorplan #51.
9. **Floor summaries, outdoors.** Average temperature per floor, cars, pools, decks.
10. **3D as an option.** Real demand, split, and it drags performance complaints behind it.

## What we nailed

- **Asks 1 and 2 by design.** Draw inside HA, pickers for areas and entities, vector output. Nothing is ever re-rendered.
- **Ask 7.** One plan in `.storage`, every card reads it. Easy Floorplan keeps its plan in the dashboard config. No runtime network, icons inlined.
- **Photo to plan through a portable skill with a validator.** Nobody else ships one. The 0.10.3 `available` snapshot for offline device placement is unique too.
- **Organising HA from the plan.** Create areas, groups, switch-to-light automations, schedules, the per-room "In Home Assistant" box. None of the rivals writes groups or automations.
- **Dumb bulb on a smart switch as one icon.** Niche, real, undocumented elsewhere.
- **The card's state language.** Light colour and aura, motion fade, door and cover state, camera cones, tap and long press. Seven themes, one that follows HA. Dark mode is solved.
- **Ask 6 and the outdoor half of 9.** Fixed lengths, plain openings, snapping, rotation, textures, gardens, ponds, pavements.

## What we miss, by likely impact

1. **Nobody can find it.** Gap. Zero stars, custom repository, not in the HACS default list. Easy Floorplan got 587 stars in four months by posting. Features do not matter until this is fixed.
2. **No zoom or pan in the card.** Gap. Nothing in `src/card` handles wheel, pinch or drag. Ask 3, and every rival has it. A whole house on a phone in portrait is small.
3. **Nothing moves.** Gap. No `person`, `device_tracker`, mmWave or vacuum. Ask 5 is where the rivals are spending their effort.
4. **No image to trace over.** Gap, by choice. The skill covers people who will use an assistant. It leaves out those who won't, or won't share the drawing.
5. **Light realism is modest.** Weakness. An aura plus `room_glow` next to sun, skylights and a dark house with lit rooms. Ask 4 carries the emotional pull; people post screenshots of it.
6. **No card visual editor.** Weakness. `getStubConfig` exists, `getConfigElement` does not. UI-mode users expect a form. Install code softens it.
7. **No kiosk or wall-tablet mode.** Unknown. Nothing in the docs. Houseplan has it.
8. **History replay, floor summary.** Gap, lower volume.
9. **3D.** Gap, by choice, defensible.
10. **The README undersells us.** The status table still says Organise is "Sprint 4, not built" and the card "is not yet seen on a dashboard by eye". Both shipped in 0.10.0. A first-time reader is told the product is less than it is.

## Hands-on findings, this build

- **Label collision.** On the demo, "Garden" and "Garden pond" overprint each other, in the editor and in every card theme. Labels are placed at the centroid with no collision check. Small rooms next to each other will always do this.
- **Sensor value over a zone label.** The temperature "23.5" sits on the "Reading corner" label. Same cause.
- **The side panel repeats the snapping manual.** Every panel, floor or device, carries the same six-line paragraph on Alt, Shift, Ctrl and the middle button. It is noise after the first read and pushes the real fields around. It belongs in Help, or behind a "?" link.
- **Feedback is easy to miss.** Status ("Ready", "Saved") is a small line at the bottom of the side panel, far from the button that caused it.
- **Phone width.** The toolbar wraps to three rows and the plan is a stamp in the middle. The editor is desktop by decision, and Houseplan makes the same call. Fine, as long as the card gets zoom.
- **Shots baseline drift.** `npm run shots` reports 15 images differ from the local baseline. The diff is the 0.10.0 furniture grey token, not a regression. Accept the baseline.
- **Menus are clear.** Add, Draw, View, File, Help read well at a glance; Load demo in File is the right first click. The device panel is dense but complete.

## How to make it better, in order

1. **Be found.** Post in the HA community "Share your Projects" and r/homeassistant with the demo GIF and one paragraph on what no one else does (no re-render, groups and automations from the plan, photo to plan). Apply for the HACS default list. Fix the README status table first, same day. Cost: an afternoon.
2. **Zoom and pan in the card.** Wheel and pinch on the SVG viewBox, double-tap to fit, a `zoom` config key. Cost: a task. Removes the one objection every phone user will raise.
3. **Fix the two label defects.** Nudge a label off any other label or value it overlaps, or drop the smaller one below the icon. Cost: a task. Visible in the first screenshot anyone takes.
4. **Trim the side panel.** Move the snapping paragraph into Help, keep a one-line "?" link. Move the status line next to the toolbar. Cost: half a task.
5. **Presence and vacuums.** `person` and `device_tracker` as a dot that moves between rooms by area; a vacuum's position from its map entity where the integration exposes it. Cost: a sprint. This is the feature people post about now.
6. **A card config form.** `getConfigElement` with theme, floors, fade, room_glow. Cost: a task.
7. **Sun and night.** A day/night fill from `sun.sun`, lit rooms brighter than the rest. Cheap version of ask 4. Cost: a task.
8. **Trace over an image.** An optional background image in the editor, never in the card, never saved into the layout. Cost: a task. Covers people who will not use an assistant.
9. **Kiosk mode.** A `kiosk: true` config that hides everything but the plan and disables long press. Cost: half a task.
10. Leave 3D and history replay where they are.
